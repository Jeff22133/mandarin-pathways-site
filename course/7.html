    Writing characters

    请问今天号月星期昨明去学校看书

    How these characters are built

    左右 zuǒyòu · Left–right

    Split down the middle. The left piece is usually the one carrying the meaning, and it is narrowed to make room.

    你亻 + 尔汉氵 + 又说讠 + 兑

    左中右 zuǒzhōngyòu · Left–middle–right

    Three columns rather than two, so each is squeezed narrower still.

    谢讠 + 身 + 寸

    Where these characters come from

    天

    大 (a person) with a line drawn above the head: the sky overhead.

    月

    A crescent moon. As a component it can also stand for flesh or the body.

    书

    A hand gripping a brush — writing, and so a book.

    Sentence patterns

    Giving a date

    MonthDateDay of week
    七月八号星期一
    七月九号星期五

    Largest unit first, always: month, then date, then weekday — the opposite of the usual English order.

    Dates need no verb

    SubjectNoun predicate
    今天星期一。
    今天七月八号。
    昨天七月七号。

    With a date or a weekday the noun phrase IS the predicate — 是 is simply left out.

    Go somewhere, then do something

    Subject去PlaceVerb phrase
    我去学校看书。
    他去学校问老师。

    Two verbs in a row, in the order the actions happen: first you go, then you do the thing. No word joins them.

    Tone pairs

    Two-syllable words starting on tone 2 — listen for the shape, not the word.
