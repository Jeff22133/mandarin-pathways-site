    Sentence patterns

    在……呢 for right now

    Subject在Verb phrase呢
    我在学习呢。
    他在看电视呢。

    This 在 marks an action in progress. 呢 at the end is optional but very common in speech.

    吧 to soften a suggestion

    Sentence吧
    你给我打电话吧。
    我们看电视吧。
    请坐吧。

    吧 turns an instruction into a suggestion — the difference between 'sit' and 'do sit down'.

    喂 on the telephone

    SituationWhat you say
    Answering喂？
    Saying who you are喂，我是大卫。

    喂 is only for the phone. Using it face to face sounds like shouting at someone.

    也 sits before the verb

    Subject也Verb phrase
    我也喜欢看书。
    我也学习汉语。
    他也看电视。

    Never at the start of the sentence — 也 always follows the subject.

    How it works

    在…呢 marks an action happening right now

    Subject + 在 + Verb/Object (+ 呢)

    Before an action, 在 can mark that the action is in progress now. 呢 at the end reinforces the ongoing feeling and is common in conversation, but can be omitted. Compare 我学习汉语 (I study Chinese generally) with 我在学习汉语呢 (I am studying Chinese right now). Negate the ongoing action with 没(在) + Verb and normally leave off 呢.

    Do not mix this up

    我学习在汉语呢。→我在学习汉语呢。

    The progressive 在 belongs directly before the action.

    How it works

    吧 softens a suggestion or request

    Suggestion + 吧

    Put 吧 at the end when inviting someone to do something or making a gentle suggestion. It changes the force, not the basic content: 我们看电视 is a statement; 我们看电视吧 is 'let's watch television'.

    Do not mix this up

    吧我们看电视。→我们看电视吧。

    吧 closes the suggestion, so it belongs at the end.

    How it works

    也 goes before the shared action

    Subject + 也 + Verb/Adjective

    也 means 'also/too'. Put it after the subject and immediately before the verb or adjective that is shared with someone else. English can put 'too' at the end; Mandarin does not use 也 there.

    Do not mix this up

    我学习汉语也。→我也学习汉语。

    也 follows the subject and precedes the shared action.

    Where these characters come from

    日

    The sun, with a mark at its centre.

    目

    An eye, stood upright so it fits a square character.

    习

    Wings over a nest — a young bird practising flight, hence 'to practise'.
