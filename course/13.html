    Writing characters

    喂也学习上午睡觉电视喜欢给打话吧

    Where these characters come from

    习

    Wings over a nest — a young bird practising flight, hence 'to practise'.

    上

    A mark placed above a baseline: up, above.

    午

    A pestle; borrowed for the middle of the day.

    电

    Lightning streaking out of a cloud — now electricity.

    Sentence patterns

    在……呢 for right now

    Subject在Verb phrase呢
    我在学习呢。
    他在看电视呢。

    This 在 marks an action in progress. 呢 at the end is optional but very common in speech.

    吧 to soften a suggestion

    Sentence吧
    你给我打电话吧。
    我们看电视吧。
    请坐吧。

    吧 turns an instruction into a suggestion — the difference between 'sit' and 'do sit down'.

    喂 on the telephone

    SituationWhat you say
    Answering喂？
    Saying who you are喂，我是大卫。

    喂 is only for the phone. Using it face to face sounds like shouting at someone.

    也 sits before the verb

    Subject也Verb phrase
    我也喜欢看书。
    我也学习汉语。
    他也看电视。

    Never at the start of the sentence — 也 always follows the subject.

    Tone pairs

    Three-syllable words starting on tone 3.

    A third tone before another tone is a low dip, not a full fall-rise — it only rises when the syllable stands alone.

    Speaking

    On the phone

    Answer as though you had picked up the phone.

    喂，你在做什么？

    wèi, nǐ zài zuò shén me

    Hello — what are you doing?

    我在＿呢。

    你几点睡觉？

    nǐ jǐ diǎn shuì jiào

    What time do you go to sleep?

    我＿点睡觉。
