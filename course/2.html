Thanks and goodbyes

Thanking someone, replying to thanks, and saying goodbye

What you'll learn

  • Thank someone with 谢谢 and reply with 不客气

  • Say goodbye with 再见

    Where these characters come from

    口

    An open mouth, drawn from the front.

    贝

    A cowrie shell. Shells were early money, so 贝 turns up in characters about wealth and trade.

    山

    Three peaks rising from a baseline.

    小

    Three tiny marks — grains of sand. The opposite of 大.

    不

    Once a plant's root or calyx; borrowed long ago as the negative 'not'.
