    Sentence patterns

    Asking a small number with 几

    SubjectVerb / time几 + measure
    你家有几口人？
    你女儿今年几岁？
    李月家有几口人？

    几 is for numbers you expect to be small — under ten or so. A measure word always follows it.

    Numbers past ten

    TensOnesTogether
    二十二十
    二十三二十三
    九十九九十九

    Say the tens, then the ones — there is nothing between them. 二十三 is literally 'two-ten-three'.

    有 for having

    Subject有What
    我家有三口人。
    我有女儿。
    李月有朋友。

    有 covers both 'have' and 'there is'.

    Asking age with 多大

    Subject今年多大 / number 岁
    你今年多大？
    我今年二十岁。
    她今年八岁了。

    How it works

    Use 有 for what someone has

    Person/Family + 有 + Noun

    有 means 'to have'. Put the person or family first, then 有, then what they have. Ask a small number with 几 before its measure word: 你家有几口人？ Negate 有 only with 没有, never 不有.

    Do not mix this up

    我不有女儿。→我没有女儿。

    有 has its own negative form: 没有.

    How it works

    Ask and state age without 是

    多大 / 几岁; Subject + Number + 岁

    Use 几岁 for a young child, where a small number is expected, and 多大 as the general age question. State age directly as Subject + Number + 岁. Chinese does not insert 是 between the person and the age.

    Do not mix this up

    我是二十岁。→我二十岁。

    Age follows the subject directly; it is not an identity sentence with 是.

    Where these characters come from

    水

    A stream running down the middle with ripples either side.

    女

    A figure kneeling with hands crossed in front.

    了

    A swaddled child with its arms wrapped; now used grammatically for a change or a completed action.

    大

    A person standing with arms and legs stretched wide.

    Speaking

    Your family and age

    Answer with your real family and your real age — the numbers are the point.

    你家有几口人？

    nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ kǒu rén

    How many people are in your family?

    我家有＿口人。

    你今年多大？

    nǐ jīn nián duō dà

    How old are you this year?

    我今年＿岁。
