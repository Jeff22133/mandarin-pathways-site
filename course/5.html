    Writing characters

    家有口女儿几岁了今年多大一二三四五六七八九十

    Where these characters come from

    口

    An open mouth, drawn from the front.

    女

    A figure kneeling with hands crossed in front.

    儿

    A child with the top of the skull still open — hence 'child', and the -r suffix.

    几

    A low table; also borrowed to ask 'how many'.

    了

    A swaddled child with its arms wrapped; now used grammatically for a change or a completed action.

    年

    A person carrying harvested grain — one harvest, one year.

    大

    A person standing with arms and legs stretched wide.

    一

    A single tally stroke — the simplest possible count.

    二

    Two tally strokes stacked.

    三

    Three tally strokes stacked.

    四

    Four tally strokes at first; later reshaped into a mouth with breath, and kept for the number.

    五

    A crossed counting stick between two lines.

    六

    Once a simple shelter; borrowed for the number six.

    七

    Originally a cutting mark; borrowed for the number seven.

    八

    Two strokes turning away from each other — originally 'to divide', later borrowed for the number.

    九

    An arm bent sharply at the elbow; borrowed for the number nine.

    十

    A knotted cord marking a completed count of ten.

    Sentence patterns

    有 for having

    Subject有What
    我家有三口人。
    我有女儿。
    李月有朋友。

    有 covers both 'have' and 'there is'.

    Asking age with 多大

    Subject今年多大 / number 岁
    你今年多大？
    我今年二十岁。
    她今年八岁了。
