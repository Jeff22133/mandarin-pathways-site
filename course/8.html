    Sentence patterns

    Asking a price with 多少

    Thing多少钱
    这个杯子多少钱？

    How it works

    想 expresses what you would like

    Subject + 想 + Verb/Object

    Put 想 before an action to express a wish or preference: 想喝, 想吃, 想买. It is softer than a demand and is the natural form for ordering food or saying what you feel like doing. Negate it as 不想.

    Do not mix this up

    我喝想茶。→我想喝茶。

    想 comes before the action it describes.

    How it works

    Ask and answer a price

    Thing + 多少钱? / Number + 块钱

    多少钱 asks 'how much money?' Put the thing being priced first. Answer with the number followed by 块钱; in ordinary speech 块 is the common unit word for yuan. Unlike 几, 多少 does not assume a small answer.

    Do not mix this up

    多少钱这个杯子？→这个杯子多少钱？

    Name the thing first; 多少 stays in the amount slot.

    How it works

    Use 个 between this/that and a noun

    这/那 + 个 + Noun

    A demonstrative and noun normally need a measure word between them: 这 + 个 + 杯子. 个 is the general measure word introduced here. The same slot appears after a number: 一个杯子.

    Do not mix this up

    这杯子→这个杯子

    At this level, keep the measure word between 这/那 and the noun.

    Where these characters come from

    少

    小 (small) with one more stroke: smaller still, hence 'few'.

    个

    A single stalk of bamboo — which is why it became the all-purpose measure word.
