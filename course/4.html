Friends and where they're from

Asking who someone is, their nationality, and whether they're a friend or classmate

What you'll learn

    Sentence patterns

    Asking who and which

    Subject是谁 / 哪国人
    他是谁？
    她是哪国人？
    李月是谁？

    谁 asks which person, 哪 asks which one of a set. Both sit where the answer will go — the word order never changes.

    的 for belonging

    Owner的Thing / person
    我的朋友
    他的老师
    李月的名字

    Owner first, then 的, then what's owned — the reverse of English.

    呢 to bounce a question back

    Topic呢?
    她呢？
    你的老师呢？
    我的朋友呢？

    Instead of repeating the whole question, name the new topic and add 呢.

    Speaking

    Talk about people you know

    The book does this in pairs, looking at photos. Answer about someone you actually know.

    他是谁？

    tā shì shéi

    Who is he?

    他是我的＿。

    她是哪国人？

    tā shì nǎ guó rén

    What country is she from?

    她是＿人。
