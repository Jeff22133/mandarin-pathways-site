    Sentence patterns

    Asking with 什么

    SubjectVerb什么 (+ noun)
    你叫什么名字？
    你是什么人？
    李月叫什么？

    什么 sits where the answer will go — the word order of a question is the same as the statement. Nothing moves to the front as it does in English.

    The 是 sentence

    Subject(不)是Noun phrase
    李月是学生。
    我是美国人。
    我不是老师。

    Negate with 不 before 是 — there is no separate negative verb.

    Yes/no questions with 吗

    SubjectPredicate吗?
    你是老师吗？
    你是中国人吗？
    李月是学生吗？

    Say the statement, then add 吗 — the word order never changes.

    How it works

    什么 stays where the answer would go

    Subject + Verb + 什么 (+ Noun)?

    Chinese does not move 'what' to the front. Put 什么 in the same place as the missing answer: 我叫李月 → 你叫什么名字？ The statement and the question keep the same order. At this lesson, 什么名字 is the fixed chunk used to ask a name.

    Do not mix this up

    什么你叫名字？→你叫什么名字？

    什么 replaces the answer inside the sentence; it does not move to the front as English 'what' often does.

    How it works

    Use 是 to identify someone

    A + (不)是 + B

    是 links one noun to another: a person to a name, job, nationality, or category. It never changes for I, you, or they. Put 不 directly before 是 to make the identity negative: 不是. Do not add 是 before an adjective such as 好; 是 is doing the job 'A is B', not every English use of 'be'.

    Do not mix this up

    我不学生。→我不是学生。

    Negate an identity by putting 不 directly before 是.

    How it works

    Add 吗 to ask yes or no

    Statement + 吗?

    Start with a complete statement and add 吗 at the very end. Nothing else moves: 你是学生。→ 你是学生吗？ Answer by repeating the main verb: 是 / 不是, rather than relying on a separate word for yes or no.

    Do not mix this up

    你是吗学生？→你是学生吗？

    吗 is appended to the whole statement, so it belongs at the end.

    Which stroke comes first

    • Horizontal before vertical.十
    • Left-falling before right-falling.八人

    Where these characters come from

    月

    A crescent moon. As a component it can also stand for flesh or the body.

    心

    A heart with its chambers — it appears in characters about feeling and thought.

    中

    A pole through the centre of an enclosure: the middle.

    人

    A person seen from the side, two legs walking.

    Speaking

    Introduce yourself

    Answer out loud, then check one way of saying it. There is no single right answer — these are about you.

    你叫什么名字？

    nǐ jiào shén me míng zi

    What's your name?

    我叫＿。

    你是学生吗？

    nǐ shì xué sheng ma

    Are you a student?

    我＿。

    你是中国人吗？

    nǐ shì Zhōng guó rén ma

    Are you Chinese?

    我是＿人。
