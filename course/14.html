    Where these characters come from

    东

    A bundle tied at both ends; taken as the sun rising behind a tree, hence 'east'.

    西

    A bird's nest; borrowed for 'west', the opposite of 东.

    一

    A single tally stroke — the simplest possible count.

    儿

    A child with the top of the skull still open — hence 'child', and the -r suffix.

    开

    Two hands lifting the bar off a door: to open.

    车

    A cart seen from above — axle and wheels.

    回

    One enclosure inside another, drawn as a spiral: to go back.

    不

    Once a plant's root or calyx; borrowed long ago as the negative 'not'.

    少

    小 (small) with one more stroke: smaller still, hence 'few'.

    Sentence patterns

    了 for something already done

    SubjectVerb + 了Object
    我买了衣服。
    他看见了我的车。
    我去了商店。

    了 goes straight after the verb when the action is finished.

    Three-syllable words starting on tone 4.
