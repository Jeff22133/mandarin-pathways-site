HSK 2.0 · Standard Course 1

What is this?

This track follows the HSK 2.0 level-1 textbook's 15-lesson order — 148 new words, 206 items counting proper nouns and compounds. The app's spaced-review bands use the newer HSK 3.0 lists, so a few of these words actually sit in HSK 3.0 bands 2–4. /path is an alternative thematic track built on HSK 3.0 band 1. Every dialogue and exercise in this course is original — nothing here is officially endorsed by, or affiliated with, the textbook's publisher.
