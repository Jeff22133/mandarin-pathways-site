About HSK 5

HSK 5 covers 1059 words. Each one here comes with pinyin, meanings, native-speaker audio, stroke-order practice for its characters, and spaced review that brings it back before you forget it.

Words at this level include 可 (kě — (prefix) can; may; able to; -able), 冠军 (guàn jūn — champion), 之中 (zhī zhōng — inside), 接触 (jiē chù — to touch), 集团 (jí tuán — group), 冒 (mào — to emit), 试图 (shì tú — to attempt), 熊 (xióng — bear), 不足 (bù zú — insufficient), 评估 (píng gū — to evaluate), 除非 (chú fēi — only if (..., or otherwise, ...)), 饮料 (yǐn liào — drink), 搜 (sōu — to search), 寻求 (xún qiú — to seek; to look for), 过度 (guò dù — excessive), 码头 (mǎ tóu — dock), 寸 (cùn — a unit of length), 挣 (zhèng — to struggle to get free), 睡眠 (shuì mián — sleep), 钢琴 (gāng qín — piano), 摘 (zhāi — to take), 打架 (dǎ jià — to fight), 饮食 (yǐn shí — eating and drinking), 将要 (jiāng yào — will), 等候 (děng hòu — to wait), 内在 (nèi zài — inner), 数目 (shù mù — amount), 占有 (zhàn yǒu — to have), 设想 (shè xiǎng — to imagine), 斜 (xié — inclined), 直线 (zhí xiàn — straight line), 加热 (jiā rè — to heat), 承办 (chéng bàn — to undertake), 总数 (zǒng shù — total), 单一 (dān yī — single), 号召 (hào zhào — to call), 确立 (què lì — to establish), 工作日 (gōng zuò rì — workday), 农产品 (nóng chǎn pǐn — agricultural produce), 大熊猫 (dà xióng māo — giant panda (Ailuropoda melanoleuca)).
