About HSK 1

HSK 1 covers 506 words. Each one here comes with pinyin, meanings, native-speaker audio, stroke-order practice for its characters, and spaced review that brings it back before you forget it.

Words at this level include 的 (de — of; ~'s (possessive particle)), 和 (hé — (joining two nouns) and; together with; with (Taiwan pr. [hàn])), 上 (shàng — (bound form) up; upper; above; previous), 他们 (tā men — they), 里 (lǐ — lining), 最 (zuì — most), 你们 (nǐ men — you (plural)), 找 (zhǎo — to try to find), 一起 (yī qǐ — in the same place), 孩子 (hái zi — child), 问 (wèn — to ask; to inquire), 拿 (ná — to hold), 电话 (diàn huà — telephone), 路 (lù — road), 干 (gàn — tree trunk), 学 (xué — to learn), 回家 (huí jiā — to return home), 远 (yuǎn — far), 元 (yuán — currency unit (esp. Chinese yuan)), 六 (liù — six), 看见 (kàn jiàn — to see), 来到 (lái dào — to arrive; to come), 读 (dú — to read out; to read aloud), 关 (guān — mountain pass), 坏 (huài — bad), 菜 (cài — dish (type of food)), 一半 (yī bàn — half), 考试 (kǎo shì — to take an exam), 茶 (chá — tea), 贵 (guì — expensive), 在家 (zài jiā — to be at home), 是不是 (shì bù shì — is or isn't), 右边 (yòu bian — right side), 那儿 (nà r — there), 晚饭 (wǎn fàn — evening meal), 外国 (wài guó — foreign (country)), 爱好 (ài hào — to like; to be fond of; to take pleasure in; to be keen on), 放假 (fàng jià — to have a holiday or vacation), 半天 (bàn tiān — half of the day), 里边 (lǐ bian — inside).
