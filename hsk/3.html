About HSK 3

HSK 3 covers 953 words. Each one here comes with pinyin, meanings, native-speaker audio, stroke-order practice for its characters, and spaced review that brings it back before you forget it.

Words at this level include 把 (bǎ — to hold; to grasp), 总 (zǒng — general; overall), 方式 (fāng shì — way; manner; style; mode; pattern), 下来 (xià lai — to come down), 台 (tái — platform), 父亲 (fù qīn — father), 某 (mǒu — some), 深 (shēn — deep (lit. and fig.)), 地区 (dì qū — local), 祝 (zhù — to pray for), 集中 (jí zhōng — to concentrate), 空 (kōng — empty), 直 (zhí — straight), 各种 (gè zhǒng — every kind of; all kinds of; various), 当地 (dāng dì — local), 咖啡 (kā fēi — coffee (loanword)), 退 (tuì — to retreat; to withdraw), 合理 (hé lǐ — rational; reasonable; sensible; fair), 优势 (yōu shì — superiority), 试验 (shì yàn — experiment), 专门 (zhuān mén — specialist), 进步 (jìn bù — progress; improvement), 话题 (huà tí — subject (of a talk or conversation)), 离婚 (lí hūn — to divorce), 补 (bǔ — to repair), 保安 (bǎo ān — to ensure public security), 至今 (zhì jīn — so far), 直播 (zhí bō — (TV, radio) to broadcast live), 安装 (ān zhuāng — to install), 考验 (kǎo yàn — to test), 球迷 (qiú mí — fan (ball sports)), 缺少 (quē shǎo — lack), 创新 (chuàng xīn — to bring forth new ideas; to blaze new trails), 缺点 (quē diǎn — weak point), 金牌 (jīn pái — gold medal), 散步 (sàn bù — to take a walk; to go for a walk), 民间 (mín jiān — among the people), 彩色 (cǎi sè — color), 表格 (biǎo gé — form), 专题 (zhuān tí — specific topic (addressed by a book, lecture, TV program etc)).
