About HSK 6

HSK 6 covers 1123 words. Each one here comes with pinyin, meanings, native-speaker audio, stroke-order practice for its characters, and spaced review that brings it back before you forget it.

Words at this level include 与 (yǔ — and), 利 (lì — sharp), 学会 (xué huì — to learn), 娱乐 (yú lè — to entertain), 档案 (dàng àn — file), 埋 (mái — to bury), 平衡 (píng héng — balance), 智慧 (zhì huì — wisdom), 扎 (zhā — to prick), 主角 (zhǔ jué — leading role; lead), 领袖 (lǐng xiù — leader), 同一 (tóng yī — identical; the same), 外科 (wài kē — surgery (branch of medicine)), 直升机 (zhí shēng jī — helicopter), 争夺 (zhēng duó — to fight over), 时装 (shí zhuāng — fashion), 晓得 (xiǎo de — to know), 超出 (chāo chū — to exceed), 通话 (tōng huà — to hold a conversation), 演奏 (yǎn zòu — to perform on a musical instrument), 没收 (mò shōu — to confiscate), 起点 (qǐ diǎn — starting point), 笑声 (xiào shēng — laughter), 追究 (zhuī jiū — to investigate), 罢工 (bà gōng — a strike), 电动 (diàn dòng — electric-powered), 明日 (míng rì — tomorrow), 出面 (chū miàn — to appear personally), 必将 (bì jiāng — inevitably), 站台 (zhàn tái — platform (at a railway station)), 秘书长 (mì shū zhǎng — secretary-general), 外观 (wài guān — outward appearance), 自言自语 (zì yán zì yǔ — to talk to oneself), 升值 (shēng zhí — to rise in value), 王后 (wáng hòu — queen), 待会儿 (dāi huì r — in a moment), 反问 (fǎn wèn — to ask (a question) in reply), 总量 (zǒng liàng — total), 笔试 (bǐ shì — written examination), 好容易 (hǎo róng yì — (idiomatic usage) with great difficulty).
