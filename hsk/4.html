About HSK 4

HSK 4 covers 972 words. Each one here comes with pinyin, meanings, native-speaker audio, stroke-order practice for its characters, and spaced review that brings it back before you forget it.

Words at this level include 而 (ér — and), 定 (dìng — to set), 非 (fēi — to not be; not), 停下 (tíng xià — to stop), 翻 (fān — to turn over), 稳定 (wěn dìng — steady), 既 (jì — already), 摆 (bǎi — to arrange), 刺激 (cì jī — to provoke; to irritate; to upset; to stimulate; to excite), 方案 (fāng àn — plan), 放松 (fàng sōng — to relax; to slacken; to loosen), 兵 (bīng — soldiers), 类型 (lèi xíng — type; kind; category), 寄 (jì — to send), 巧克力 (qiǎo kè lì — chocolate (loanword)), 姐妹 (jiě mèi — sisters), 腰 (yāo — waist), 相反 (xiāng fǎn — opposite), 引导 (yǐn dǎo — to guide), 反映 (fǎn yìng — to mirror), 薄 (bó — meager), 舒适 (shū shì — cozy), 吃惊 (chī jīng — to be startled), 比分 (bǐ fēn — score), 招呼 (zhāo hu — to call out to), 抽烟 (chōu yān — to smoke (a cigarette, tobacco)), 沉重 (chén zhòng — heavy), 尺寸 (chǐ cun — size; dimensions; measurements (esp. of clothes)), 诗人 (shī rén — bard), 利息 (lì xī — interest (on a loan)), 符号 (fú hào — symbol; mark; sign), 台阶 (tái jiē — steps), 征求 (zhēng qiú — to solicit), 晚点 (wǎn diǎn — (of trains etc) late), 闹钟 (nào zhōng — alarm clock), 改正 (gǎi zhèng — to correct), 感兴趣 (gǎn xìng qù — to be interested), 率先 (shuài xiān — to take the lead), 看不起 (kàn bu qǐ — to look down upon), 窗台 (chuāng tái — window sill).
