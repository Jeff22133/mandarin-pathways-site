About HSK 2

HSK 2 covers 750 words. Each one here comes with pinyin, meanings, native-speaker audio, stroke-order practice for its characters, and spaced review that brings it back before you forget it.

Words at this level include 啊 (a — modal particle ending sentence, showing affirmation, approval, or consent), 可能 (kě néng — might (happen)), 公司 (gōng sī — company; firm; corporation), 或 (huò — maybe), 全 (quán — all), 道 (dào — road), 离开 (lí kāi — to depart; to leave), 活动 (huó dòng — to exercise), 卡 (kǎ — (loanword) card), 行为 (xíng wéi — action), 气 (qì — gas; air), 变成 (biàn chéng — to change into), 满 (mǎn — to fill), 后来 (hòu lái — afterwards; later), 样子 (yàng zi — appearance), 日子 (rì zi — day), 喂 (wèi — hey), 怎么办 (zěn me bàn — what's to be done), 词 (cí — word), 篇 (piān — sheet), 大部分 (dà bù fen — in large part), 爬 (pá — to crawl), 红色 (hóng sè — red (color)), 同事 (tóng shì — colleague; co-worker), 放心 (fàng xīn — to feel relieved), 感动 (gǎn dòng — to move (sb)), 碰 (pèng — to touch), 做法 (zuò fǎ — way of handling sth), 云 (yún — (classical) to say), 今后 (jīn hòu — hereafter), 对面 (duì miàn — (sitting) opposite), 爱人 (ài ren — spouse (PRC)), 饱 (bǎo — to eat till full), 得出 (dé chū — to obtain (a result); to arrive at (a conclusion)), 实习 (shí xí — to practice), 护照 (hù zhào — passport), 休假 (xiū jià — to go on vacation; to have a holiday; to take leave), 日报 (rì bào — daily newspaper), 饺子 (jiǎo zi — dumpling), 方便面 (fāng biàn miàn — instant noodles).
