About HSK 7-9

HSK 7-9 covers 5606 words. Each one here comes with pinyin, meanings, native-speaker audio, stroke-order practice for its characters, and spaced review that brings it back before you forget it.

Words at this level include 之 (zhī — (possessive particle, literary equivalent of 的)), 砍 (kǎn — to chop), 姿势 (zī shì — posture), 尚未 (shàng wèi — not yet; still not), 幸好 (xìng hǎo — fortunately), 艾滋病 (ài zī bìng — AIDS (loanword)), 火焰 (huǒ yàn — blaze), 验证 (yàn zhèng — to inspect and verify), 恐慌 (kǒng huāng — panic), 同类 (tóng lèi — similar), 俊 (jùn — smart), 落地 (luò dì — to fall to the ground), 蒸 (zhēng — to evaporate), 总的来说 (zǒng de lái shuō — generally speaking), 恐吓 (kǒng hè — to threaten), 侵害 (qīn hài — to encroach on), 地域 (dì yù — area), 牢固 (láo gù — firm), 手术室 (shǒu shù shì — operating room), 天桥 (tiān qiáo — overhead walkway), 摇摆 (yáo bǎi — to sway), 施行 (shī xíng — to put in place), 往返 (wǎng fǎn — to go back and forth; to go to and fro; to do a round trip), 出游 (chū yóu — to go on a tour), 高昂 (gāo áng — to hold (one's head) high), 不起眼 (bù qǐ yǎn — unremarkable), 新陈代谢 (xīn chén dài xiè — metabolism (biology)), 赞叹 (zàn tàn — to exclaim in admiration), 盘算 (pán suàn — to plot), 不懈 (bù xiè — untiring), 息息相关 (xī xī xiāng guān — closely bound up (idiom); intimately related), 利害 (lì hai — terrible), 大名鼎鼎 (dà míng dǐng dǐng — grand reputation), 共性 (gòng xìng — overall character), 根深蒂固 (gēn shēn dì gù — deep-rooted (problem etc)), 阎王 (Yán wáng — (Buddhism) Yama, the King of Hell), 纯朴 (chún pǔ — simple and honest; unsophisticated (variant of 淳朴)), 增收 (zēng shōu — to increase revenue), 国徽 (guó huī — national emblem), 无情无义 (wú qíng wú yì — completely lacking any feeling or sense of justice (idiom); cold and ruthless).
