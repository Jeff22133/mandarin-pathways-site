Service Phrases

What staff actually say at the counter — and how to answer them like a local.

  • 在这儿吃还是带走？
    zài zhèr chī hái shi dài zǒu?

    for here or to go?

  • 扫这个码
    sǎo zhè ge mǎ

    scan this code

  • 需要袋子吗？
    xū yào dài zi ma?

    do you need a bag?

  • 微信还是支付宝？
    Wēi xìn hái shi Zhī fù bǎo?

    WeChat Pay or Alipay?

  • 一共三十八块
    yí gòng sān shí bā kuài

    that's 38 yuan altogether

  • 请稍等
    qǐng shāo děng

    please wait a moment

  • 马上就好
    mǎ shàng jiù hǎo

    it'll be ready right away

  • 卖完了
    mài wán le

    sold out

  • 请出示健康码
    qǐng chū shì jiàn kāng mǎ

    please show your health QR code

  • 发票要吗？
    fā piào yào ma?

    do you need the receipt/invoice?

  • 加您微信
    jiā nín wēi xìn

    let's add you on WeChat

  • 试衣间在那边
    shì yī jiān zài nà biān

    the fitting room is over there

  • 这是找您的钱
    zhè shì zhǎo nín de qián

    here's your change

  • 满一百减二十
    mǎn yì bǎi jiǎn èr shí

    spend 100, get 20 off

  • 关注我们的公众号
    guān zhù wǒ men de gōng zhòng hào

    follow our official account

  • 请签名
    qǐng qiān míng

    please sign here

  • 慢走，欢迎下次再来
    màn zǒu, huān yíng xià cì zài lái

    take care, come again

  • 自助点单
    zì zhù diǎn dān

    order here yourself (self-service ordering)

  • 先扫码后付款
    xiān sǎo mǎ hòu fù kuǎn

    scan the code first, then pay

  • 有会员卡吗？
    yǒu huì yuán kǎ ma?

    do you have a membership card?

  • 可以试吃吗？
    kě yǐ shì chī ma?

    can I try a sample?

  • 不用，谢谢
    bú yòng, xiè xie

    no need, thanks
