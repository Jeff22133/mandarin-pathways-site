Signs & Notices

Storefronts, restrooms, elevators — decode the signs you'll actually see on the street.

  • 入口
    rù kǒu

    entrance — this way in

  • 出口
    chū kǒu

    exit — this way out

  • 营业中
    yíng yè zhōng

    open now — come on in

  • 暂停营业
    zàn tíng yíng yè

    temporarily closed

  • 营业时间
    yíng yè shí jiān

    opening hours — check before you go

  • 禁止吸烟
    jìn zhǐ xī yān

    no smoking allowed here

  • 禁止拍照
    jìn zhǐ pāi zhào

    no photos allowed here

  • 小心地滑
    xiǎo xīn dì huá

    caution: floor is wet and slippery

  • 请勿触摸
    qǐng wù chù mō

    please don't touch this

  • 男厕所
    nán cè suǒ

    men's restroom — enter here

  • 女厕所
    nǚ cè suǒ

    women's restroom — enter here

  • 收银台
    shōu yín tái

    cashier — pay here

  • 会员价
    huì yuán jià

    members-only price

  • 打折区
    dǎ zhé qū

    discounted items in this section

  • 免费无线网络
    miǎn fèi wú xiàn wǎng luò

    free Wi-Fi available here

  • 紧急出口
    jǐn jí chū kǒu

    emergency exit — use only in an emergency

  • 请排队
    qǐng pái duì

    please line up here

  • 请勿打扰
    qǐng wù dǎ rǎo

    do not disturb

  • 电梯故障
    diàn tī gù zhàng

    elevator is out of order

  • 自助结账
    zì zhù jié zhàng

    self-checkout — scan and pay yourself

  • 24小时监控
    èr shí sì xiǎo shí jiān kòng

    this area is under 24-hour camera surveillance
