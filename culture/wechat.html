WeChat etiquette

WeChat (微信) is the default channel for nearly everything in China — messaging, payments, official notices — so its etiquette carries real weight. Adding a new contact usually happens by scanning a QR code (扫码加我) right after meeting, sometimes replacing a business card exchange entirely in casual or younger settings.

朋友圈 (Moments, WeChat's feed) functions as ongoing relationship maintenance — liking and commenting on acquaintances' posts is a small, expected gesture of goodwill, and its absence over time can be read as fading interest. Because Moments can be segmented by audience, don't assume everyone sees the same version of someone's feed. A relationship that only exists through mutual likes is sometimes called 点赞之交 ('like-only acquaintance') — a mild joke about a shallow tie.

Red packets (微信红包) are a common way to say thanks, split a bill, or celebrate — including playful 拼手气红包, group packets randomly split among whoever taps fastest. In work group chats, acknowledge a boss's or group announcement with 收到 ('received') rather than staying silent, and try not to leave a voice message hanging without a reply — 已读不回 ('read but didn't reply') is a real, commonly cited social offense. Keep sensitive or political content out of group chats, and be mindful that forwarding chain messages or spam to group chats is generally unwelcome.

Useful phrases

  • 加一下微信
    jiā yí xià wēi xìn

    let's add each other on WeChat

  • 扫码加我
    sǎo mǎ jiā wǒ

    scan the code to add me

  • 发红包
    fā hóng bāo

    send a red packet

  • 收到
    shōu dào

    received; got it (acknowledging a message)

  • 已读不回
    yǐ dú bù huí

    read but didn't reply (etiquette complaint)

  • 拼手气红包
    pīn shǒu qì hóng bāo

    lucky-draw red packet (randomly split in a group)

Quick tips

  • •Reply 收到 to a boss's or group announcement rather than staying silent.
  • •Don't leave a voice message unanswered — 已读不回 is a real etiquette complaint.
  • •Keep political or sensitive content out of group chats; a small red packet is a welcome way to say thanks.
